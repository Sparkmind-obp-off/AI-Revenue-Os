# AFFILIATE OS — SCOPE & MVP BOUNDARY

## MVP In Scope
- affiliate product discovery/catalog
- product/offer selection
- content/distribution planning
- affiliate link/reference management
- click tracking
- conversion tracking
- commission tracking
- revenue recording
- basic attribution
- performance dashboard
- next-action recommendations
- audit trail

## Prototype In Scope
- landing/dashboard
- opportunity/product discovery
- offer detail
- content/distribution workspace
- tracking/performance view
- revenue summary
- recommendation view
- simulated end-to-end journey

## Out of Scope
- autonomous publishing
- autonomous financial actions
- payout engine
- full CRM
- full social scheduler
- native mobile app
- every affiliate network integration

## MVP Acceptance
One complete attributable affiliate revenue loop must work deterministically with safe retries, tenant isolation, auditability, and test evidence.
