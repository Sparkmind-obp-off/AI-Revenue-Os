## Canonical Relationship
`Merchant → Affiliate Program → Affiliate Product → Affiliate Offer → Affiliate Link → Click → Conversion → Commission → Revenue`

## Deterministic Truth
Clicks/conversions may originate from external systems, but canonical commission and revenue records must be validated and stored internally with source references, timestamps, currency, amount, status, tenant ownership, and idempotency protection.

## Attribution
Every attributable revenue record should be traceable to the conversion/order and upstream distribution/content/offer context where evidence exists.
