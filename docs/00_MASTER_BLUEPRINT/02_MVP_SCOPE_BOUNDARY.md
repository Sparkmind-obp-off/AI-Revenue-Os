# AI REVENUE OS — MVP SCOPE & BOUNDARY

**Version:** 1.0

## MVP Objective
Membuktikan satu revenue loop yang dapat dijalankan, diukur, dan ditelusuri end-to-end melalui Vertical #01: Affiliate OS.

## In Scope
- tenant/user foundation
- opportunity and offer concepts
- product/offer catalog
- distribution reference
- click/conversion attribution
- commission and revenue records
- basic performance reporting
- audit/event traceability
- human approval boundaries
- prototype/demo experience

## Out of Scope
- autonomous money movement
- full payout engine
- full CRM
- full social scheduler
- mobile app
- multi-vertical abstraction beyond proven shared semantics
- advanced autonomous agents

## MVP Gate
MVP hanya dianggap complete jika revenue loop memiliki deterministic financial state, attribution evidence, authorization controls, idempotency, tests, dan production evidence.

## Prototype Boundary
Prototype boleh memakai mock data/local state untuk demo. Prototype tidak boleh menulis production financial state atau menggunakan production credentials.
