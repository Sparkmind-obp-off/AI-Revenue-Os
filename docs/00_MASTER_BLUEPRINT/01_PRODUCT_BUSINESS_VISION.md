# AI REVENUE OS — PRODUCT & BUSINESS VISION

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Blueprint  
**Parent:** `AI_REVENUE_OS_MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md`

## 1. Product Definition
AI Revenue OS adalah platform operating system untuk membangun, menjalankan, mengukur, dan mengoptimalkan revenue loop lintas vertical.

## 2. Problem
Revenue activity biasanya tersebar antara research, content, distribution, affiliate/commerce tools, tracking, spreadsheet, dan analytics. Akibatnya opportunity sulit diprioritaskan, attribution lemah, dan keputusan revenue sulit diaudit.

## 3. Product Promise
Menghubungkan signal → opportunity → offer → distribution → transaction → revenue → measurement → next action dalam satu operating model.

## 4. Target Users
- Solo operator / creator
- Affiliate marketer
- Digital product operator
- Small business growth operator
- Revenue team / operator

## 5. North Star
**Reliable revenue loop before autonomous intelligence.**

## 6. Success Metrics
- Revenue loop completion rate
- Valid attributable conversions
- Revenue captured correctly
- Time from signal to action
- Operator approval rate
- Repeat/retention revenue
- Data quality and reconciliation rate

## 7. Business Model Direction
Revenue OS dapat dikembangkan sebagai SaaS/platform; vertical pertama menjadi bukti product-market fit dan revenue-loop fit sebelum ekspansi vertical.

## 8. Non-Goals
Tidak membangun seluruh CRM, commerce suite, social scheduler, payment processor, atau autonomous agent platform pada tahap awal.
